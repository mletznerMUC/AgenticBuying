# Research note — State of AdCP and AAMP (agentic buying protocols)

- **Topic:** Current state of AdCP (Ad Context Protocol) and AAMP (IAB Tech Lab), their relationship, and adjacent protocol efforts
- **Date of research / verification:** 2026-08-04
- **Researcher:** research-analyst agent
- **Note on sourcing:** Several primary pages (adcontextprotocol.org, iabtechlab.com, prnewswire.com, digiday.com, ppc.land, iabcanada.com) returned HTTP 403 through the research proxy. Where the page itself could not be fetched, claims were verified via search-index summaries of those URLs and cross-checked against at least one second source; these rows are marked "(via search index)". GitHub pages were fetched directly.

## Summary

Two protocol families now dominate agentic ad buying. **AdCP (Ad Context Protocol)** launched 2025-10-15 from a supply-side coalition (Yahoo, PubMatic, Scope3, Optable, Swivel, Triton Digital), is governed by the nonprofit **AgenticAdvertising.org** (117+ members), runs over MCP/A2A, and reached general availability on its v3 spec line in 2026 (latest tagged release v3.1.x, active weekly release cadence). **AAMP** is the **IAB Tech Lab's "Agentic Advertising Management Protocols"** — an umbrella framework (named 2026-02-26) that extends existing standards (OpenRTB, OpenDirect 2.1, AdCOM, VAST) with agentic layers; it shipped Buyer/Seller Agent SDKs in AAMP 2.0 (April 2026) and released AAMP 2.3 on 2026-07-30 with pricing-provenance, privacy-diligence gates, and Amazon Bedrock/Databricks compatibility. The two efforts began as a publicly reported standards rivalry (late 2025) but are increasingly described by analysts as operating at different layers of the stack; no formal interoperability agreement between AgenticAdvertising.org and IAB Tech Lab was found. Real transactions exist on both sides (NBCU/FreeWheel/RPA over MCP for AdCP-style buying; Kochava StationOne open beta on AAMP), but trade press in July 2026 characterizes overall adoption as "inevitable, but incremental," with governance outpacing volume. Important correction for this site: AAMP officially stands for "Agentic Advertising **Management** Protocols," not "Agentic Advertising & Marketing Protocols" as currently rendered in `site/aamp.html`.
