Detailed comparison

Origins and backers

AdCP (Ad Context Protocol) launched on October 15, 2025 from a largely supply-side coalition — Yahoo, PubMatic, Scope3, Optable, Swivel, and Triton Digital — and is now stewarded by the AgenticAdvertising.org nonprofit. AAMP (Agentic Advertising Management Protocols) is the IAB Tech Lab's initiative: its components shipped under separate names from November 2025 and were unified under the AAMP name on February 26, 2026.

Source: PPC Land (AdCP launch); PPC Land (AAMP naming) · last verified 2026-08-04

Design philosophy

The clearest documented contrast is architectural. AdCP defines new, purpose-built agent task schemas carried over MCP (Anthropic's Model Context Protocol) or Google's A2A (Agent2Agent), and describes itself as parallel to OpenRTB rather than a replacement. AAMP instead layers agentic capabilities onto the IAB Tech Lab's existing standards stack — OpenRTB, OpenDirect, AdCOM, VAST, and taxonomies — while also using MCP and A2A as agent interfaces. Both stacks therefore share the same underlying transports but differ in whether the domain vocabulary is new or inherited.

Source: dev.to analysis; Star Global; Perform Digital · last verified 2026-08-04

Governance and licensing

AdCP is governed by AgenticAdvertising.org, a Delaware-incorporated trade association (501(c)(6) status pending) with four equally weighted voting classes (brands, agencies, publishers, technology providers); the Prebid community maintains the reference sell-side implementation, and the spec is Apache 2.0 licensed with express patent grants and an IPR policy. AAMP is developed by IAB Tech Lab's Agentic Task Force with member working groups; its repositories are versioned independently, and licensing is mixed — notably, the Agentic Real Time Framework (ARTF) is published under AGPL v3.

Source: AdCP GitHub repository; IAB Tech Lab; IABTechLab repositories · last verified 2026-08-04

Maturity and release cadence

AdCP is on a v3.1.x line with a weekly-plus release cadence (latest tagged release v3.1.10, August 4); secondary sources describe the 3.0 line as generally available in 2026, though no primary GA announcement has been located. AAMP's most recent milestone is version 2.3 (July 30, 2026), following 2.0 in April 2026 (one secondary source says June; April is better sourced). The contents of AAMP 2.1 and 2.2 are not documented in accessible sources. reported

Source: AdCP GitHub releases; Adgentek glossary; PR Newswire (AAMP 2.3); IAB Tech Lab (AAMP 2.0) · last verified 2026-08-04

Transactions and adoption evidence

Both sides can point to real activity, of different kinds. On the AdCP side, NBCUniversal, agency RPA, FreeWheel, and Newton Research announced the first cross-platform agentic media buys in January 2026, with buy-side agents negotiating sell-side agents over MCP for live NFL playoff linear inventory; AgenticAdvertising.org membership is reported at between 104+ and 117+ companies (exact count unverified). On the AAMP side, Kochava's StationOne offers an open beta for standards-compliant agentic workflows without real spend, and AAMP 2.3 added Amazon Bedrock and Databricks compatibility. Trade press in July 2026 characterized overall agentic buying adoption as "inevitable, but incremental," with governance outpacing transaction volume on both stacks.

Source: NBCUniversal; Adgentek statistics; PPC Land (Kochava); MediaPost · last verified 2026-08-04

Rivalry or complementarity?

In late 2025, trade press framed the two efforts as a standards "showdown": AdCP's launch was reported to have divided the industry, and IAB Tech Lab's January 2026 agentic roadmap was described as showing "no love for AdCP."

Source: Digiday; PPC Land; The Current · last verified 2026-08-04

speculative Whether the relationship today is competition or complementarity is a matter of analyst disagreement, not settled fact. Some analysts argue the two operate at different levels of the stack and are designed to work alongside each other; others continue to frame it as a race between competing standards. No formal interoperability agreement, liaison, or joint working group between AgenticAdvertising.org and IAB Tech Lab is documented as of August 4, 2026 — complementarity claims are analyst interpretation, not organizational commitments.

Source: Star Global (complementary view); Perform Digital and Ripley Media (rivalry view) · last verified 2026-08-04
