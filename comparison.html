Detailed comparison

Origins and backers

AdCP (Ad Context Protocol) launched on October 15, 2025 from a largely supply-side coalition — Yahoo, PubMatic, Scope3, Optable, Swivel, and Triton Digital — and is now stewarded by the AgenticAdvertising.org nonprofit. AAMP (Agentic Advertising Management Protocols) is the IAB Tech Lab's initiative: its components shipped under separate names from November 2025 and were unified under the AAMP name on February 26, 2026.

Source: PPC Land (AdCP launch); PPC Land (AAMP naming) · last verified 2026-08-04

Design philosophy

The clearest documented contrast is architectural. AdCP defines new, purpose-built agent task schemas carried over MCP (Anthropic's Model Context Protocol) or Google's A2A (Agent2Agent), and describes itself as parallel to OpenRTB rather than a replacement. AAMP instead layers agentic capabilities onto the IAB Tech Lab's existing standards stack — OpenRTB, OpenDirect, AdCOM, VAST, and taxonomies — while also using MCP and A2A as agent interfaces. Both stacks therefore share the same underlying transports but differ in whether the domain vocabulary is new or inherited.

Source: dev.to analysis; Star Global; Perform Digital · last verified 2026-08-04

reported That architectural contrast is not a difference in what each family covers, and two 2026 analyses argue that comparisons routinely read it as one. They disagree on how much the two overlap. IAB Tech Lab, writing about its own protocol, describes AAMP as addressing planning, discovery, negotiation, ordering, governance, audiences, and campaign execution — the same functional stages AdCP covers — and argues that treating the Agentic Real Time Framework (ARTF) as the whole of AAMP is the most common error in third-party comparisons. No Fluff Advisory, whose author discloses that he co-leads an AdCP working group, puts the real overlap more narrowly: both families negotiate and book deals, and the differences lie above and below that plane. Each is the position of a party to the comparison, so both are recorded here rather than adjudicated; neither is adopted as this site's own account of the overlap.

Source: IAB Tech Lab — clarifying the AAMP scope (undated); No Fluff Advisory, 31 July 2026 · last verified 2026-08-20

Governance and licensing

AdCP is governed by AgenticAdvertising.org, a Delaware-incorporated trade association with four equally weighted voting classes (brands, agencies, publishers, technology providers) and bylaws providing for a 40-director elected board; the Prebid community maintains the reference sell-side implementation, and the spec is Apache 2.0 licensed with express patent grants and an IPR policy. reported Its own sources disagree on whether its 501(c)(6) status is pending or granted. AAMP is developed by IAB Tech Lab's Agentic Task Force with member working groups and organized under three pillars — Agentic Foundations, Agentic Protocols, and Trust and Transparency. Its repositories are versioned independently and licensing is mixed: most are Apache 2.0, the Agentic Real Time Framework (ARTF) is AGPL v3, and the registry example carries no license.

Source: AdCP GitHub repository; AgenticAdvertising.org — About; IAB Tech Lab; IABTechLab repositories · last verified 2026-08-04

Maturity and release cadence

AdCP is on a v3.1.x line with a weekly-plus release cadence (latest tagged release v3.1.16, August 19, 2026), with 3.1 as the current stable minor version and a v3.0.x maintenance line running alongside it through v3.0.25. Its documentation refers to 3.0 having reached general availability and records v2 as fully deprecated on August 1, 2026, though no date for the GA milestone itself is published. announced A v3.2.0-beta.0 pre-release opened a 3.2 line on August 17, 2026 and reached beta.3 by August 19; no stable 3.2 release exists yet. AAMP's most recent announced milestone is version 2.3 (July 30, 2026), following 2.0 in April 2026. reported The AAMP 2.0 date remains disputed — several secondary sources say April, one says June, and IAB Tech Lab's own post carries no dateline. The two version histories are also counted differently: AdCP tags the specification itself, while AAMP's public milestone numbers sit above component repositories that version independently, which is why 2.1 and 2.2 exist as repository tags but not as announced protocol releases.

Source: AdCP GitHub releases; AdCP documentation; PR Newswire (AAMP 2.3); IAB Tech Lab (AAMP 2.0); AAMP GitHub repository · last verified 2026-08-04; the AdCP release lines verified 2026-08-20 and the AAMP component version tags 2026-08-18

Transactions and adoption evidence

Both sides can point to real activity, of different kinds, and the evidence on each side is now thicker than a membership list. On the AdCP side there are five documented deployments — a count that mixes one-off transactions with standing products, and is not a count of live integrations: the first agent-to-agent buy between Scope3 and Swivel agents on LG Ads inventory in October 2025; PubMatic and Butler/Till's agentic connected-TV campaign in December 2025; the NBCUniversal, RPA, FreeWheel and Newton Research cross-platform buys in January 2026, including live football playoff linear inventory; Optable's Audience Agent inside PubMatic AgenticOS from March 2026; and the DanAds and Sigma Software Sales Agent for Publishers with theScore and RTL AdAlliance from June 2026. On the AAMP side, PMG has the Buyer Agent architecture and Agent Registry in production inside its Alli operating system, IAB Tech Lab's Agent Registry is open for anyone to register, and Kochava's StationOne offers an open beta for standards-compliant workflows without real spend. Trade press in July 2026 still characterized overall agentic buying adoption as "inevitable, but incremental," with governance outpacing transaction volume on both stacks.

Source: MediaPost (first buy); PubMatic; NBCUniversal; ExchangeWire; PR Newswire (PMG); PPC Land (Kochava); MediaPost · last verified 2026-08-04

Rivalry or complementarity?

In late 2025, trade press framed the two efforts as a standards "showdown": AdCP's launch was reported to have divided the industry, and IAB Tech Lab's January 2026 agentic roadmap was described as showing "no love for AdCP."

Source: Digiday; PPC Land; The Current · last verified 2026-08-04

speculative Whether the relationship today is competition or complementarity is a matter of analyst disagreement, not settled fact. Some analysts argue the two operate at different levels of the stack and are designed to work alongside each other; others continue to frame it as a race between competing standards. No formal interoperability agreement, liaison, or joint working group between AgenticAdvertising.org and IAB Tech Lab is documented as of August 4, 2026 — complementarity claims are analyst interpretation, not organizational commitments. Two observations bear on the reading: IAB Tech Lab's AAMP standards page does not mention AdCP at all, and one of the analyses arguing for complementarity itself notes that there is no joint technical roadmap between the two, only parallel ones.

Source: Star Global (complementary view); Perform Digital and Ripley Media (rivalry view) · last verified 2026-08-04
