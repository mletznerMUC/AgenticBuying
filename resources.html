Specs, articles, talks & repos

Primary sources and further reading on agentic buying, AdCP, and AAMP.

Updated 2 September 2026 · the 2 September refresh re-checked every source listed here and added four, one of them replacing a claim the site previously declined to publish; each list carries its own verification date

Claims are badged by evidence strength; unbadged statements are verified against their cited source.

  • shipped released or in production
  • announced committed, not yet shipped
  • reported secondary sources only, or disputed
  • speculative interpretation, not settled fact

This page lists the sources the rest of the site is built on: the specifications and announcements published by the two standards bodies, the trade press and analyst pieces that cover them, and the adjacent protocols that AdCP (Ad Context Protocol) and AAMP (Agentic Advertising Management Protocols) depend on or sit beside. Where a source is secondary or where sources disagree, the pages citing it say so.

Some of the publishers below returned HTTP 403 or timed out for the research agent, so their claims were verified through search-index summaries or syndicated copies and cross-checked against at least one further source rather than read directly. At the September 2, 2026 check that applied to pubmatic.com, businesswire.com, globenewswire.com, and martech.org. One link below is not merely blocked but gone: the Samba TV launch release now returns HTTP 404. The links are otherwise unaffected.

Adjacent protocol efforts worth tracking

None of the following are ad-buying protocols. MCP and A2A are the transports that AdCP and AAMP run over; the rest are payment and checkout protocols for agent-executed transactions, listed here as adjacent efforts worth tracking rather than as parts of either ad protocol.

  • MCP — Model Context Protocol

    Anthropic's protocol for agent-to-tool communication, and the transport layer both AdCP and AAMP build on. It is also the protocol behind the ad-platform MCP servers listed under Tools.

    Source: AdCP GitHub repository; dev.to analysis · last verified 2026-09-02

  • A2A — Agent2Agent

    Google's agent-to-agent transport. It is a named agent interface in AAMP and a supported transport in AdCP, so a buy-side and a sell-side agent can reach each other over either A2A or MCP.

    Source: dev.to analysis · last verified 2026-09-02

  • AP2 — Agent Payments Protocol

    Google's payments and governance layer for agent transactions, built on signed "mandates" that record what an agent was authorized to buy. It launched on September 16, 2025 with more than 60 partners including PayPal, Mastercard, American Express, Adyen, and Coinbase, and is relevant here as a candidate payment rail for agent-executed media buys.

    Source: Google Cloud · last verified 2026-09-02

  • ACP — Agentic Commerce Protocol

    A checkout and commerce protocol from OpenAI and Stripe, live in ChatGPT Instant Checkout. It covers the consumer side of an agent transaction and is described in the protocol comparisons as adjacent to ad-funded agentic commerce.

    Source: Orium · last verified 2026-09-02

  • Visa TAP, Coinbase x402, Mastercard Agent Pay

    Further agent-payment protocols that the comparative write-ups place at different layers and describe as typically composed with one another rather than chosen exclusively. reported Their individual shipped status varies between shipped and announced and was not resolved per protocol during verification, and both write-ups are secondary sources. Neither names Mastercard Agent Pay: they cover Visa TAP and x402 in detail, so the Mastercard entry rests on no cited source and is retained here only as a pointer to look for one.

    Source: Payram; Crossmint · last verified 2026-09-02
