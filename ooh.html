Out-of-home as an agentic channel

A channel deep dive on OOH (out-of-home) and DOOH (digital out-of-home): which parts of the agentic buying protocols reach the medium today, what has actually been piloted, and where a one-to-many medium fits the protocols' one-to-one primitives badly.

What the protocols cover today

announced AdCP's (Ad Context Protocol) first out-of-home-specific specification surface sits in the 3.2 pre-release line, not in a stable release. Between August 20 and September 1, 2026, pre-releases v3.2.0-beta.4 through v3.2.0-beta.10 widened the line into channel-specific delivery, and two of those items name the medium directly: digital-out-of-home compliance, and posting evidence for static out-of-home. The same stretch of the line adds connected-TV experience profiles and audio VAST delivery, so out-of-home arrives as one of several channel extensions rather than as a standalone module. No stable 3.2 release existed as of September 2, 2026, so none of it is a shipped surface a buyer can build against.

Source: AdCP CHANGELOG.md; AdCP GitHub releases · last verified 2026-09-02

That the medium reaches the specification through a pre-release line rather than through the seven negotiation surfaces of AdCP 3.1 is the fact this page is organized around: the channel's protocol coverage is newer than the channel's programmatic infrastructure, and the two do not yet meet. The release tracker carries the version detail, and the AdCP and AAMP pages carry each protocol's general scope.
