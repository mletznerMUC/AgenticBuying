# Research note — AdCP and AAMP monthly refresh

- **Topic:** Re-verification of the site's AdCP/AAMP claims, developments since the last note, and the open questions carried forward
- **Date of research / verification:** 2026-08-04
- **Researcher:** research-analyst method, monthly Research Refresh workflow
- **Previous note:** [`adcp-aamp-baseline-2026-08-04.md`](adcp-aamp-baseline-2026-08-04.md) (*State of AdCP and AAMP*, 2026-08-04)
- **Note on sourcing:** This cycle reached several publishers that returned HTTP 403 to the previous
  cycle — **iabtechlab.com and prnewswire.com were fetched directly this time**, which is why a
  number of claims move from "via search index" to primary. Still blocked at verification:
  `ppc.land`, `iabcanada.com`, `sigma.software`, `businesswire.com` (403 or timeout). The GitHub
  REST API was used directly, which resolves the previous cycle's ambiguity about release years.

## Summary

The previous note was written the same day as this refresh, so almost nothing had time to *change*;
the value of this cycle is in **re-verification**, and re-verification turned up more than expected.
Reaching the primary sources that were previously proxied through search-index summaries corrected
eight claims and closed five of the eight open questions. The two most consequential corrections:
the AdCP specification now spans **seven** negotiation surfaces, not six (a **Brand** surface was
missing), and the "3.0 GA" claim the site carried as `reported` is **confirmed by primary
documentation** — `docs.adcontextprotocol.org` states that "v2 is unsupported as of 3.0 GA and fully
deprecated on August 1, 2026 (UTC)", which also surfaces a development three days before this run.
On the adoption side the site's central caution — that FreeWheel/NBCUniversal was the *only*
confirmed production AdCP deployment — is **no longer true**: four further AdCP deployments verify
(PubMatic/Butler/Till, Optable/PubMatic AgenticOS, DanAds/Sigma with theScore and RTL AdAlliance,
and the Prebid Sales Agent), and PMG has AAMP's Buyer Agent and Agent Registry in production inside
its Alli operating system. Against that, re-verification also *weakened* three things: the
AgenticAdvertising.org 501(c)(6) status is now a disagreement between two primary sources rather
than a settled "pending"; the FreeWheel "AdCP server" attribution is not in the NBCUniversal release
at all; and the HorizonOS Blu buying-agent claim is not supported by either source the site cites
for it.
