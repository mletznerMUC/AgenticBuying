- **Topic:** Re-verification of the claims due this cycle, a priority read of two new AdCP-vs-AAMP
  comparison sources, and the open questions carried forward
- **Date of research / verification:** 2026-08-20
- **Researcher:** Research Refresh workflow — verification shards (`_verdicts/`) plus a discovery
  pass (`_discoveries/discoveries.md`), applied by the refresh's apply stage
- **Previous note:** [`adcp-aamp-2026-08-18.md`](adcp-aamp-2026-08-18.md) (*AdCP and AAMP refresh*,
  2026-08-18)
- **Note on sourcing:** Every source the verification pass was given was reached; no claim lost its
  source this cycle. Five claims were due — the three `high`-volatility AdCP release-line and
  membership claims and the two `high`-volatility AAMP component-release claims. The discovery pass
  read both priority articles in full and then re-checked their factual assertions against primary

## Summary

Two things changed materially, one of them a **correction to a claim the site had been publishing
since 4 August**.

The release-line claims moved as expected for `high`-volatility facts: AdCP's latest 3.1.x tag is
now **v3.1.16 (19 August 2026)**, the parallel maintenance line reached **v3.0.25 (19 August)**, and
the 3.2 pre-release line ran on through **beta.1 to beta.3 (18–19 August)** without a stable release.
On the AAMP side, **buyer-agent and seller-agent both tagged v2.4.2 on 18 August**, and IAB Tech
Lab's press-release page still names no "AAMP 2.4," so that version framing stays `reported`.

The correction is the membership count. The site said AgenticAdvertising.org "publishes no count of
its own." That was checked against the About and Membership sub-pages in two prior cycles, and never
against the bare homepage — where **both adcontextprotocol.org and agenticadvertising.org state
"127+"** members. The site now carries the organization's own figure, badged as verified, with the
two caveats the check actually supports: the figure is undated by the organization, and no page
enumerates a membership of that size. The 104+/117+ secondary range is retained only as the
secondary reading it always was. This is the cycle's most useful result and its most uncomfortable
one: the claim was not wrong because a source moved, it was wrong because the check had been aimed
at the wrong page.

The priority topic — two new comparison sources, one from a **disclosed-partisan analyst**
(No Fluff Advisory, whose author co-leads an AdCP working group) and one from **IAB Tech Lab
itself** — produced a genuine gap in the site's comparison page rather than a factual error. Both
argue that published AdCP/AAMP comparisons understate the two protocols' functional overlap, and
they disagree about how much overlap there is: IAB Tech Lab claims seven shared functional stages,
No Fluff Advisory confines it to deal negotiation and booking. Audited against IAB Tech Lab's own
list of five recurring comparison errors, the site was clear on four and exposed on the fifth: its
"at a glance" Scope row and Design philosophy section contrasted the two architectures without ever
saying they address the same territory. Both characterizations are now recorded on
`comparison.html`, attributed and badged `reported`, and neither is adopted as the site's own.

Three further primary-source checks came out of that reading. The registry API now returns **30
agents, of which exactly one is a buying agent** — the same single buying agent a 31 July snapshot
recorded, so all registry growth has been sell-side. **Agentic Audiences contradicts itself**: AAMP
2.3's announcement calls v1.0 "ready for transactions" while the repository has no tagged release
and its spec file, inside a directory named `specs/v1.0`, is headed "Draft v0.1." And of AAMP 2.3's
announced platform support, **Bedrock AgentCore is documented in the seller-agent repository while
Databricks is not mentioned in either agent repository** — a Databricks-authored repository built on
the official SDKs exists, but outside the IABTechLab organization and unlinked to the announcement.

No open questions closed this cycle. Two were advanced on evidence, three new ones opened.

## Open questions

Nothing closed this cycle; the priority topic took precedence over the carried backlog.

### Advanced on evidence, still open

- **Is "127+" precise?** The organization states the figure on two homepages but does not date it,
  and no page lists members at that count, so it cannot be reconciled name by name. The site
  publishes it as the organization's own figure and says both of those things.
- **AAMP 2.3's dateline** remains disputed between IAB Tech Lab's press-release index (27 July) and
  PR Newswire, MarTechCube, and PPC Land (30 July). No Fluff Advisory's essay places 2.3 two days
  after AdCP's v3.1.8 tag of 28 July, consistent with 30 July — a fourth secondary corroboration of
  the already-favored date, not a primary dateline. The site still publishes 30 July and records
  the discrepancy.

### Newly opened

- **The IAB Tech Lab scope-clarification post carries no publication date** anywhere on the page —
  no byline date, dateline, or "last updated" marker. Checked directly. IAB Tech Lab's
  press-release index and its RSS/sitemap were not checked for a machine-readable date; worth
  trying next cycle.
- **Whether Agentic Audiences' "v1.0" names a spec generation or a maturity level.** A spec file in
  a directory named `specs/v1.0` is headed "Draft v0.1," and no IAB Tech Lab statement addressing
  the discrepancy was found. Until one is, the site records the contradiction rather than picking a
  reading.
- **Whether `databricks-industry-solutions/databricks-iab-aamp-buy-sell` is the Databricks
  integration AAMP 2.3 credits.** The repository is real and states it is built on the official IAB
  AAMP SDKs, but no IAB Tech Lab or Databricks announcement names it, and the AAMP 2.3 press release
  names Databricks without a repository pointer.

### Carried forward unchanged

- **AAMP 2.0's date is not primary-datelined.** April is supported by four secondary sources plus a
  GitHub errata note on the `seller-agent` v2.0 tag, dev.to says June, and IAB Tech Lab's own post
  carries no visible date — so the claim stays `reported`. Not investigated this cycle.
- **Whether an "AAMP 2.4" exists as a protocol version.** Component repos are now at v2.4.2 and IAB
  Tech Lab's press-release page still names no 2.4 release, so the version framing stays `reported`.
- **The AAO's 501(c)(6) status is contested**, not merely pending: the spec repo says "pending," the
  organization's own About page says it is one. No IRS filing has been checked. Not investigated
  this cycle.
- **What AdCP 3.2 ships as stable, and when.** The line has moved from beta.0 to beta.3 in three
  days without a stable release; the deprecations in its changelog will matter to implementers. The
  whole line stays `announced`.
- **Fox Broadcasting's agentic transactions** — the only claim on record is a third-party
  characterization in a MediaPost comment, so nothing is published. Not investigated this cycle.
