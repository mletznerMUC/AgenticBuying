- **Run date:** 2026-09-02

- **Scope:** Full re-verification of every claim in [`claims.yaml`](claims.yaml) —

## Summary

This was a full-manifest cycle rather than a volatility-sharded one: all 63 claims
on the books were re-checked against their sources, and two were added. 62 of the 63
reached their sources. The one that did not is the ARTF latency figure, whose sole
source, martech.org, returned HTTP 403 to repeated fetches — it moves from
`announced` to `reported` and keeps its 2026-08-04 date rather than acquiring a
verification date this run did not earn.

Eight claims changed. Four are the ordinary drift of a fast-moving spec: AdCP's
release lines advanced to v3.1.19 and v3.0.26, its 3.2 pre-release line ran from
beta.3 to beta.10 without a stable release, the agent registry grew from 30 to 32
agents, and the organization's own membership figure moved from 127+ to 132+. Four
are corrections to the site rather than movement in the world:

- The **AdCP roadmap list** the site carried is no longer traceable to any cited
  source, and the functionality it named now ships in 3.1. It drops to
  `speculative` and is recorded as withdrawn rather than deleted.
- **Adform's open-standards statement** is more specific than the site said: the
  ExchangeWire piece names AAMP 2.0, ARTF, and a deployed MCP server, which the
  site had described as naming no protocol, product, or timeline.
- The **UCP-to-Agentic-Audiences rebrand** is not in the January 2026 roadmap
  press release the site cited for it. Only the standards page attests it,
  retrospectively and undated. That half of the claim drops to `reported`.
- The **AAMP 2.3 dateline discrepancy** is gone. IAB Tech Lab's press-release index
  now reads 30 July, matching every other channel, and the RSS feed gives a
  machine-readable `pubDate` of 30 July 2026.

Two source-integrity problems surfaced. The **Samba TV launch release** returns
HTTP 404; it was the only source for AdCP's $10,000-per-year founding commitment
and for the three-module framing of v2.0.0, so both are now badged `reported`
wherever they appear rather than quietly dropped. And the **Mastercard Agent Pay**
entry on `resources.html` is named by neither of its two cited write-ups.

Three developments are genuinely new and four open questions closed — including
Fox, where a MediaPost comment the site declined to publish turns out to have a
primary source behind it: Fox Corporation's own 17 June 2026 release, naming a
Simulmedia partnership for automated linear-television buying.

- **AAMP 2.3's dateline (27 vs. 30 July 2026).** Closed at 30 July. Every channel
  agrees, and the RSS feed gives `pubDate: Thu, 30 Jul 2026 12:24:17 +0000`. No
  source states 27 July; the earlier reading has no traceable origin.
- **The scope-clarification post's publication date.** Closed at 19 August 2026,
  from RSS `pubDate` and a consistent `post-sitemap.xml` `lastmod`.
- **Fox Broadcasting's agentic transactions.** Closed and upgraded: Fox
  Corporation's own 17 June 2026 release, corroborated by Adweek, a MediaPost
  article, Simulmedia's blog, and decodeTV. Now published on `tools.html`.
- **Whether the AdCP roadmap list is current.** Closed, negatively: it is not
  traceable to any cited source and the site no longer carries a roadmap.

### Newly open

- **Why the Samba TV launch release disappeared, and whether the $10,000/year
  founding commitment is documented anywhere else.** It is repeated widely in
  secondary coverage but was not located in a reachable primary source this cycle.
- **Whether Mastercard Agent Pay belongs in the adjacent-protocols list at all.**
  Neither cited write-up names it. A source naming it, or its removal, is the next
  step.
- **Whether AdCP's 3.2 line has an intended stable date.** The line has now run
  eleven pre-releases over sixteen days and is still widening in scope, not
  converging.
