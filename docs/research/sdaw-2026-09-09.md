# SDAW read from the norm — 9 September 2026

- **Run date:** 2026-09-09
- **Scope:** Supersedes the SDAW side of [`sdaw-2026-09-08.md`](sdaw-2026-09-08.md).
  That note's SDAW findings were reconstructed from a 2011–2013 open-source
  toolchain because the specification was believed unobtainable. The norm is now
  held, and several of those findings were wrong — including the one the page's
  central argument rested on.
- **Related pages:** `sdaw.html` (rewritten), `ooh.html` (deep-dive section
  corrected), `docs/DESIGN.md` (new evidence state), `CLAUDE.md` and
  `.claude/agents/*` (binding source rules).
- **Provenance.** The primary source is **SDAW version 004, revision date
  11 June 2025, B|A|M Bundesverband Aussenmedien**, with appendices A to E, plus
  the 2027 posting-date calendar and four production SDAW files from one lessee
  (number 368, fiscal year 2017). None of these is publicly retrievable. They are
  registered in [`sources/local-context.md`](sources/local-context.md); derived
  extracts are in [`sources/sdaw/`](sources/sdaw/).

## Summary

The organizing finding **changes direction**. The previous note held that the gap
runs both ways and that SDAW's weakest steps were exactly where AdCP's pre-release
work is strongest. On the norm, SDAW's process coverage is the *more* complete of
the two: 29 record types carry the chain from panel master data through option,
booking, posting order and proof of posting to **billing**, a step neither agentic
protocol models. What SDAW lacks is a way to call any of it, and any concept of
digital delivery. The gap is **transport and digital data model, not process
scope**.

The single largest correction is **panel identity**. The previous note recorded
lessee-scoped site numbering as SDAW's structural weakness and the reason no
market-wide panel identifier could be handed to an agent. SDAW has the QID: an
eight-digit identifier from the central FAW database, independent of the lessee,
bound to the panel's coordinate, direction, height and carrier form. The
identifier exists; it is the protocols that have nowhere to put it.

Three findings are new rather than corrected: **inventory-side product
exclusions** at a granularity neither protocol models, a **network-trading layer**
added in 2019 that is the closest structural match to an AdCP product or AAMP
package in the whole mapping, and a **negative finding about digital delivery**
that can be stated exactly — across 4,278 lines the norm never once says DOOH,
API, JSON, impression, playout or proof of play.

This note also introduces a fifth badge state, **`primary-nonpublic`**, because
the site had no honest way to say "verified, and you cannot check it yourself".

### 4. What did not change

The AdCP and AAMP findings from the 2026-09-08 note stand and were **not**
re-verified in this cycle, which read the norm rather than the protocol surfaces.
Their manifest rows keep their 2026-09-08 dates: `sdaw-panel-identity-no-target`,
`sdaw-inventory-geo-no-target`, `sdaw-cycle-grid-no-target`,
`sdaw-audience-currency-unnamed`, `sdaw-adcp-static-ooh-draft`,
`sdaw-adcp-release-state-rc1`, `sdaw-opendirect-nearest-model`,
`sdaw-idooh-lineage`.

One protocol-side consequence is worth recording even so, as an interpretation
rather than a finding: AdCP's out-of-home metrics accept a **seller-modeled
estimate** as an estimation-basis tier, and SDAW's appendix D 06 **forbids**
lessee-set performance values. A German lessee moving onto AdCP today would be
declaring, as a seller estimate, a number its own market rules prohibit it from
estimating. Badged `speculative` on the page.

## Open questions

- **The transcription backlog.** Eight of 29 record types, the complete `STA`,
  `FRE`, `LWT` and `VSA` field lists, the 20 main and 152 sub site classes, the
  236 product codes, the 34 dated decades and 52 weeks, and the appendix E series
  between 2003 and 2024. Each extract's `pending` array is the worklist. This is
  a gap in this project's reading, not in the norm.
- **`validation-report.md` has not been generated.** The run that produced these
  extracts had no access to the source folder, so no production file could be
  opened. `validate.py` was exercised end to end against a synthetic fixture and
  reproduces planted defects and record geometry correctly, which verifies the
  code and not the extracts. The first run with folder access must generate the
  report and commit it.
- **Whether B\|A\|M treats 63 or 64 as the correct `LWT` record length**, and
  which line of the norm the contradiction sits in.
- **Whether the three appendix A anomalies are errata or deliberate** — `SES`,
  the doubled `SD`, and `VD CF` alongside `FD`.
- **SDAW exchange volume today**, and by which lessees. Maintenance through June
  2025 is established; volume is not.
- **Whether a modernization toward an API-based successor is under discussion in
  the market.** No published timetable was located. Carried on the page as one
  `speculative` line with no detail.
- Unchanged from 2026-09-08: whether any SDAW-to-IDOOH or SDAW-to-OpenOOH mapping
  exists; whether AdCP's static out-of-home draft reaches a released schema;
  whether AAMP's out-of-home intent produces an artifact; whether any DACH media
  owner has an agentic out-of-home implementation.
