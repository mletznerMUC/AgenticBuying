# OOH and DOOH in agentic buying — 4 September 2026

- **Run date:** 2026-09-04
- **Scope:** New topic note, first pass. Builds the evidence base for `ooh.html`
  (already scaffolded on the site with a placeholder "Sections in preparation"
  block). Covers: what AdCP and AAMP say about OOH/DOOH today; the adjacent
  standards an agentic OOH buy depends on; vendors and media owners active in
  the channel; dated worldwide proofs of concept; the medium's structural
  problems and proposed solutions; and cross-channel framing.
- **Related pages:** `ooh.html` (target), `adcp.html`, `aamp.html`,
  `releases.html`, `comparison.html`, `resources.html`.

## Summary

Two clearly separate layers of "OOH support" exist and this note keeps them
apart deliberately. The **legacy programmatic rails** — OpenRTB's DOOH and Qty
objects, AdCOM's DOOH object, OpenDirect 2.1's DOOH extension, the OpenOOH
venue taxonomy — are mature, IAB Tech Lab/OAAA-governed, and predate agentic
buying by years; they already let a DSP bid on a physical screen and estimate
an audience-per-play. The **agentic layer** sitting on top of them is thin and
new: AdCP's only DOOH-specific schema work (placement attributes, static-OOH
posting evidence, a duration-weighted share-of-voice definition) lives in an
unreleased 3.2 pre-release line that reached release-candidate status on 3
September 2026 but has shipped nothing stable; AAMP's own standards page
describes out-of-home as a *future* extension of an initiative currently
scoped to digital advertising, and an independent analysis found AAMP's
OpenDirect-based agent SDKs treat DOOH as "media-type enums and demo catalog
entries" with no dedicated transaction path. Genuine agent-to-agent OOH buying
POCs exist and are dated — most solidly a Broadsign/Draft Digital/Global
Netherlands campaign on AdCP (27 May 2026) and a VIOOH Seller Agent handling
100+ curated deal packages (H1 2026, Cannes Lions announcement 26 June) — but
every one located is European or North American; no verified agent-mediated
OOH buying was found in APAC, LATAM, or MEA, where activity found is ordinary
programmatic/direct-sales expansion. No Ströer-specific agentic-OOH
announcement was found despite a targeted search.

### 5. Medium-specific problems and proposed solutions

| Problem | How it's expressed technically | Proposed solution / current state | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One-to-many/broadcast impressions, no user identifier, no click, no cookie | An OOH "impression" is structurally a multiplier applied to a play, not a per-user event | OpenRTB's `Qty` object carries a fractional, publisher-disclosed impression multiplier per play; AdCP's static-OOH contract (3.2 pre-release) models delivery as "a period-level modeled audience estimate," explicitly not a play-event count | shipped (Qty object) / announced (AdCP's explicit static-OOH framing) | [OpenRTB 2.6](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/InteractiveAdvertisingBureau/openrtb2.x/main/2.6.md); [AdCP CHANGELOG](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adcontextprotocol/adcp/main/CHANGELOG.md) |
| Measurement latency and proof-of-play/proof-of-posting verification | Digital-OOH proof rests on sensor/log-file confirmation of plays; static/classic OOH has no play events at all and settles on "proof-of-posting" instead | AdCP's 3.2 pre-release line adds a static-OOH `ooh_metrics` block with posting periods and "static-out-of-home posting evidence," explicitly modeled on OAAA conventions rather than invented from scratch | announced | [AdCP CHANGELOG](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adcontextprotocol/adcp/main/CHANGELOG.md) |
| Inventory addressability: screen vs. venue vs. network | Buyers need to express intent at different granularities (a specific screen, a venue type, or a whole network) | The OpenOOH Venue Taxonomy (v1.2.1, Feb 2026) gives a shared three-tier vocabulary (11 parent categories) for venue-level targeting; AdCP's 3.2 line references "versioned OpenOOH identifiers" directly in its placement schema | reported (taxonomy version/date) / announced (AdCP's reference to it) | [OAAA/GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/10/3235451/0/en/oaaa-releases-updated-openooh-taxonomy-to-advance-industry-standardization-transparency.html); [AdCP CHANGELOG](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adcontextprotocol/adcp/main/CHANGELOG.md) |
| Loop/share-of-voice and slot scheduling | A DOOH screen typically loops a set of ads; a buyer's "share" is a fraction of loop time, not a guaranteed exclusive slot | AdCP's 3.2 line defines "DOOH share of voice as duration-weighted time share," aligns "contracted and delivered units," and adds "seller-packaged slot contiguity parameters" and post-merge "slot-to-loop validation" | announced | [AdCP CHANGELOG](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adcontextprotocol/adcp/main/CHANGELOG.md), `3.2.0-rc.0` and `3.2.0-beta.10` entries |
