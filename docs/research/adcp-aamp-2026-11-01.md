# AdCP and AAMP refresh — 2026-11-01

*Research Refresh run, apply stage. Verified 2026-11-01. Inputs: twelve verification shards
(68 claims) and one discovery pass. Supersedes [`adcp-aamp-2026-10-01.md`](adcp-aamp-2026-10-01.md)

## Summary

A volatility-sharded cycle: 68 claims checked, 57 reaching their sources and 11 not.

The headline is release cadence on the AdCP side and a pause on the AAMP side. **AdCP's stable
line moved from v3.2.1 to v3.2.8** (28 October 2026, marked Latest), passing v3.2.5, v3.2.6 and
v3.2.7. The 3.1 maintenance line reached v3.1.31 (30 October) and the 3.0 line v3.0.28 (7 October).
A **3.3 pre-release line opened** on 22 October and reached v3.3.0-beta.3 on 31 October; its notes
say to use it in development and staging, not production. None of these changelogs was read, so
this note records tags and dates only.

On the AAMP side, **no component repository has tagged since 18 August**, now about eleven weeks.
The 3.0 spec effort continues: PPC Land reports that IAB Tech Lab **extended the OpenProposal
comment period from 22 October to 10 November 2026**. That report is secondary, and the repository
still showed 22 October on 1 November. Read together, these suggest effort has moved from
component tags to the spec. That is an interpretation, not a stated plan.

Counts moved. Membership is 157+ on AgenticAdvertising.org's homepage, up from 148+. The agent
registry fell again, to 33 (27 sales), its second decline in a row.

Eleven claims changed on the evidence:

- Four are spec or count drift: the release lines, membership and the registry.
- Seven are corrections to the site:
  - The DanAds launch was 25 June, not 24 June.
  - AAO's About page now says the organization is *intended to qualify* under 501(c)(6), so the
    two sources no longer clearly contradict each other.
  - AAMP's standards page no longer carries the "formerly the LiveRamp UCP donation" wording.
  - AdCP's DOOH channel page no longer shows the proof-of-play macros this site described.
  - The AAMP standards page names neither out-of-home nor radio.
  - The OpenOOH v1.2.1 specifics could not be confirmed.
  - The SDAW page's AdCP release state had gone stale.

Three claims were re-graded on a closer reading of what their sources say:

- **PMG Alli**, from `shipped` to `announced`: the release never says "production".
- **Kochava StationOne**, from `shipped` to `announced`: secondary-only, and the beta was
  "public" after an invitation-code gate was removed, not "leaving closed beta".
- **Omnicom**, from `shipped` to `reported`: the source is a secondary report of an executive's
  statement.

One more, the Geopath/Ipsos measurement pilot, moved from `announced` to `reported` because its
sole source returned 403.

Wording corrections:

- **The first agent-to-agent buy is now disputed** rather than "not established".
  adcontextprotocol.org calls it real money and real inventory, and MediaPost calls it a
  demonstration.
- The OpenProposal "four categories" grouping was not found in the repository, which lists
  seven mutability markers instead.
- `committed_metrics` is optional ("may carry").
- The 830,000-impression figure for the Broadsign campaign was not found in the pages re-read.
- PPC Land does carry VIOOH's Cannes date.
- The static-OOH draft's "concrete start and end dates" wording was not found.

The SDAW norm folder was not reachable from this run, and no norm-level claim was touched.
