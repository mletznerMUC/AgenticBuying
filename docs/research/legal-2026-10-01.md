# Agentic buying through a legal lens — research note

**Verified:** 2026-10-01 · **Behind:** `legal.html` · **Kind:** new-topic note, not a refresh run

## Origin

The substance of `legal.html` comes from an interactive explainer, *Agentic Advertising for
Legal*, contributed to the project on 2026-10-01 as a standalone HTML file. It walked a
legal reader through nine steps of an agentic buy, mapped each step to AdCP and AAMP, and
attached a legal reading (EU and German law) and a liability map. This note records which
of its claims were re-checked against primary sources before publication, which were
corrected, and which were dropped.

The legal readings themselves are interpretation and are published under a single
`speculative` scope statement at the top of the page and of the liability map, not badged
claim by claim (`docs/DESIGN.md` §5, rule 1). The statutes and judgments they cite were
checked to resolve at their official URLs (gesetze-im-internet.de, EUR-Lex ELI, curia).

### Corrected

- **`idempotency_key` is optional, not required,** on `create_media_buy` (minimum 16
  characters). The explainer said it was required on all changes. The page now says it is
  optional and recommends requiring it by contract.
- **`input-required` is not a `create_media_buy` response** in the current reference; the
  three responses are `completed`, `working`, `submitted`.
- **`get_products` returning "proposals"** is not stated in the 3.1.21 introduction; the page
  says products with pricing, targeting and forecasts.

### Dropped (not located in the AdCP documentation read)

`preview_creative`, `list_creatives`, `calibrate_content`, `deactivate_signal`, and the
delivery qualifiers `viewability_standard`, `completion_source`, `attribution_methodology`.
They may exist in deeper reference pages; they wait for a run that finds them.

## Cross-page consequence — open

AAMP 3.0 post-dates the 2026-09-15 refresh. `legal.html` is the only page that names it;
`aamp.html`, `releases.html`, `comparison.html` and the home changelog still describe the
2.4.x state. The next refresh should pick up the `aamp-3-0-openproposal` manifest row and
bring those pages forward.
