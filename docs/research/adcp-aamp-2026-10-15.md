## Summary

**Nothing on the site was found to be wrong in substance except one AAMP 3.0 detail. The AdCP
release lines kept moving, as they do every cycle.** Of 22 claims, 19 reached their sources. Six
changed: five are drift in the direction the site already described, and one is a correction.

**AdCP 3.2 took three patches in its first twelve days as a stable line.** v3.2.2 (4 October),
v3.2.3 (7 October) and v3.2.4 (12 October, now marked Latest) followed v3.2.1 of 1 October. The
apply stage read their changelog sections directly. All three are labelled patch changes: v3.2.2
is mostly documentation fixes. v3.2.3 carries a security erratum, GHSA-2pm6-6mc8-8xcm (adcp#7820),
that changes how signing rules apply over A2A, and clarifies that `authorized_agents[].url` is an
agent's full protocol endpoint URL rather than its origin. v3.2.4 adds a principal-layer
walkthrough and URL-canonicalization conformance vectors. Both maintenance lines also tagged: 3.1
reached v3.1.27 (8 October). 3.0, whose latest tag had been v3.0.26 of 21 August, took v3.0.27
(1 October) and v3.0.28 (7 October). The 3.1 and 3.0 contents were not read.

**Counts rose.** AgenticAdvertising.org's homepage now states 154+ members (148+ on 1 October).
Only that homepage was re-read; adcontextprotocol.org and the named members were not. The AdCP
agent registry API returns 36 agents (30 sales, 3 signals, 1 creative, 1 buying, 1 uncategorized),
up from 34. That reverses the one-agent decline the last note recorded, and the growth is all on
the sales side.

**One correction: OpenProposal's "four categories".** The site said AAMP 3.0's OpenProposal draft
has 71 fields in four categories: merchandising, optimization, commitment and reconciliation. The
OpenProposal README, re-read this cycle, describes seven mutability markers instead (immutable,
seller-set, selectable, settable, supplied, requestable, derived), and the four categories could not
be found. Everything else in the claim held: 22 September announcement, `v3.0-draft-1`, comment to
22 October, 71 fields with three needing a round trip, and line items mapped to OpenDirect 2.1, the
Deals API or OpenRTB 2.6. The four categories were removed from the manifest and from `legal.html`.
`legal.html` also leaned on the reconciliation category in its step-8 table and bullet, and both
now say the draft's coverage of reconciliation is not established.

**One source came back.** lbbonline.com, which returned HTTP 403 on 15 September and 1 October,
was reachable. It re-confirms VIOOH's H1 2026 Seller Agent figure (100+ curated DOOH deal-package
requests, managed service, announced 26 June at Cannes) and the plan for a Q3 2026 self-serve
version with AdCP and AAMP registration. The page carries no completion news. No separate
completion search was run, so both VIOOH rows stay `reported` but take this run's date.

**Three sources stayed unreachable:** pubmatic.com (Clubtails), businesswire.com (InMobi) and
adquick.com. All three claims were already `reported` and keep their prior dates.

**Partial re-checks did not earn a date.** Four SDAW gap rows (panel identity, inventory geo,
cycle grid, audience currency) were re-checked only on their AdCP side, where the static-ooh draft
still reads as the site describes. Their AAMP, OpenDirect, Deals API and OpenRTB halves were not
re-fetched. The OpenDirect-nearest-model row's field-level details could not be read in the
OpenDirect spec. These five keep their 2026-09-08 dates so they stay due, and the manifest records
what was re-read. By contrast, the IDOOH lineage row was fully re-read and takes this run's date.
Neither source states its "deliberately distinct, with a stated mapping intent" wording, so on
`sdaw.html` that sentence now carries a `reported` badge it previously lacked.
