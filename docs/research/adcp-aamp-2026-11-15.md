## Summary

Twenty claims were due this cycle.

- **Fifteen reached their cited source.** Five did not: the Geopath/Ipsos pilot, the VIOOH rollout
  and AdQuick returned HTTP 403, and EUR-Lex returned empty content (HTTP 202) for both legal
  dates, the Product Liability Directive deadline and AI Act Art. 50.
- **Nothing on the site was found to be wrong.** Four claims moved because the facts moved, all in
  the direction the site already described. These were AdCP's release lines (counted twice, in the
  general row and the SDAW row), the membership figure and the registry count.
- **Two badges changed.** The PLD transposition deadline and the AI Act Art. 50 application date
  on `legal.html` moved from unbadged to `reported`. Nothing suggests either fact is wrong. The
  cause is that this run could not read the official text, and the Digital Omnibus question is
  still open. The previous verification date (2026-10-01) is kept. No date was advanced for an
  unread source.

Release lines and counts:

- **AdCP's stable line is v3.2.11** (13 November 2026), after v3.2.10 (10 November) and v3.2.9
  (9 November). The 3.1 line is at **v3.1.33** (9 November) and the 3.3 pre-release line at
  **v3.3.0-beta.6** (13 November). The 3.0 line still has no tag after v3.0.28 (7 October).
  The contents of v3.2.2 to v3.2.11 and of beta.6 were not read.
- **Membership is 162+ on both homepages**, up from 159+.
- **The agent registry rose to 36** (29 sales, 4 signals, 1 creative, 1 buying, 1 untyped). That is
  one more sales agent than on 9 November.
- **AAMP's component repositories still have no tag after 18 August**, now about thirteen weeks.
  The release pages show no year, so the 2026 dating rests on earlier checks.
- **OpenProposal is still at v3.0-draft-1** five days after the reported extended comment close
  (10 November), and the repository still shows the original 22 October close. No first revision

On out-of-home: the 3.2.1 changelog's `dooh_placement_attributes`, `sales-dooh`, duration-weighted
