Agentic Buying, explained and tracked

AI agents increasingly discover, negotiate, and transact media buys on behalf of advertisers — in production, with real money. This site tracks that shift — with a focus on the two emerging protocol families, AdCP and AAMP — and keeps every claim sourced and dated.

Updated 20 August 2026 · the 20 August refresh re-checked AdCP's release lines and the AAMP component tags and added the developments below; every other claim carries the date it was last verified in its own source line

Claims are badged by evidence strength; unbadged statements are verified against their cited source.

  • shipped released or in production
  • announced committed, not yet shipped
  • reported secondary sources only, or disputed
  • speculative interpretation, not settled fact

What changed

  1. 19 August 2026

    Both AdCP release lines moved again: v3.1.16 replaced v3.1.15 as the latest tagged 3.1.x release, and the parallel v3.0.x maintenance line reached v3.0.25. announced The 3.2 pre-release line also advanced, with v3.2.0-beta.1 through v3.2.0-beta.3 following beta.0 on 18 and 19 August; there is still no stable 3.2 release. These five tags were read from the release feed's titles and dates, not from their individual changelogs.

    Source: AdCP GitHub releases · last verified 2026-08-20

  2. 18 August 2026

    AAMP's buyer-agent and seller-agent repositories both tagged v2.4.2, extending the component releases that already sat above the 2.3 milestone. reported IAB Tech Lab's press-release page still names no "AAMP 2.4," so these tags continue to document component work rather than an announced protocol version.

    Source: IABTechLab buyer-agent releases; seller-agent releases; IAB Tech Lab press releases · last verified 2026-08-20

  3. 17 August 2026

    announced AdCP published v3.2.0-beta.0, the first pre-release of a 3.2 line. Its changelog lists a promoted media-channel taxonomy, Trusted Match changes to publisher authentication and context targeting, cross-role governance enforcement, new portable and issuer-bound attestation schemas, and several deprecations — the top-level media-buy lifecycle status property, the publisher_domain filter on get_products, and inline provider credentials. It adds no eighth negotiation surface: the changes extend the existing seven. The list was read from the rendered changelog and not diffed against the schema files.

    Source: AdCP CHANGELOG.md; AdCP GitHub releases · last verified 2026-08-18

  4. 16 August 2026

    AdCP tagged v3.1.15, the most recent of five v3.1.x patch releases since 4 August (v3.1.11 through v3.1.15). Between them they covered pixel-ratio registry metadata, compliance-storyboard fixes, request-signing documentation, billing-permission handling for sellers without agent billing support, three schema fields whose inline enumerations omitted values from their canonical definitions, and a clarification that exhausting a rate-limit trip runner without observing a RATE_LIMITED response is a test-coverage gap. The v3.0.x maintenance line continued alongside it with v3.0.23 and v3.0.24 on 15 and 16 August. reported The itemized contents of those two v3.0.x patches could not be confirmed beyond their release tags and dates.

    Source: AdCP GitHub releases · last verified 2026-08-18

  5. 13 August 2026

    InMobi Advertising launched a conversational AI agent for media buyers on Buyer Hub, its self-serve deal platform, and opened Buyer Hub to agencies and managed-service demand-side platforms with role-based access controls. The coverage located does not attribute the agent to either protocol family.

    Source: Business Wire · last verified 2026-08-18

  6. 10 August 2026

    AAMP's component repositories tagged releases past the 2.3 milestone: buyer-agent v2.4.1 on 10 August, seller-agent v2.4.0 and v2.4.1 on 4 and 5 August, and iab-agentic-primitives v0.5.1 on 10 August. reported No IAB Tech Lab press release or blog post names an "AAMP 2.4," so these component tags should not be read as an announced protocol version.

    Source: IABTechLab buyer-agent releases; seller-agent releases; iab-agentic-primitives releases · last verified 2026-08-18

  7. 5 August 2026

    PubMatic added a five-step guardrail framework for buying agents to AgenticOS: permission boundaries, business rules, pre-approved asset libraries, human approval through one-time passcodes, and audit trails with drift detection. It was piloted with Rise, a Quad agency, alongside the existing AgenticOS partners Optable and AdRoll.

    Source: MediaPost; PPC Land · last verified 2026-08-18

  8. 4 August 2026

    AdCP tagged release v3.1.10, continuing a weekly-plus cadence on the v3.1.x line through July and August. A parallel v3.0.x maintenance line is still receiving releases alongside it (v3.0.22 on 24 July). Release v3.1.3 was withdrawn during that run for adding a schema field in a patch release, which the project states violates its 3.1.x release contract; the documentation pins 3.1.2 as the stable 3.1 target.

    Source: AdCP GitHub releases · last verified 2026-08-04

  9. 1 August 2026

    AdCP v2 was fully deprecated. The specification documentation states that v2 became unsupported when 3.0 reached general availability and was fully deprecated on this date; 3.1 is the current stable minor version and is additive over 3.0, so a 3.0-conformant client keeps working.

    Source: AdCP documentation — what's new in 3.0; what's new in 3.1 · last verified 2026-08-04

  10. 31 July 2026

    reported Two comparison analyses — one dated, one not — argue that published AdCP/AAMP comparisons understate how much functional ground the two protocols share, and disagree with each other about how much that is. No Fluff Advisory, whose author discloses that he co-leads an AdCP working group, confines the real overlap to deal negotiation and booking. IAB Tech Lab's own scope-clarification post puts it considerably wider — planning, discovery, negotiation, ordering, governance, audiences, and campaign execution — and names treating the Agentic Real Time Framework as the whole of AAMP as the most common error in third-party comparisons. Both are attributed positions rather than adjudicated findings, and neither is adopted here as this site's own description of the overlap. The IAB Tech Lab post carries no visible publication date, so it is grouped with the dated essay rather than dated itself.

    Source: No Fluff Advisory, 31 July 2026; IAB Tech Lab, undated · last verified 2026-08-20

  11. 30 July 2026

    IAB Tech Lab released AAMP 2.3, aimed at enterprise deployment: pricing guardrails with server-side trust verification on every price-moving path, a vendor-approval gate in the Buyer Agent tied to the IAB Diligence Platform and SafeGuard Privacy, Agentic Audiences v1.0 ready for transactions, and broader platform support — Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, Databricks, buying on Meta, and Google Ad Manager reporting.

    Source: PR Newswire; PPC Land; MediaPost · last verified 2026-08-04

Older developments — AdCP's launch, the first agent-to-agent buy, the PubMatic and Butler/Till connected-TV campaign, the NBCUniversal cross-platform buys, Optable's Audience Agent inside PubMatic AgenticOS, the DanAds and Sigma Software Sales Agent for Publishers, AAMP 2.0, PMG's Alli integration, and Kochava's open beta — are kept in the timeline tables on the AdCP and AAMP pages; Adform's open-standards commitment is on the Tools page.

How this site is maintained

An agentic development workflow builds this site: research agents verify developments against primary sources, writing agents turn them into neutral copy, and every change is reviewed before publication. Because this space moves monthly, each claim records the date it was last checked rather than implying it is permanently current.
