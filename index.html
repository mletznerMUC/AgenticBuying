Agentic Buying, explained and tracked

AI agents are beginning to discover, negotiate, and transact media buys on behalf of advertisers. This site tracks that shift — with a focus on the two emerging protocol families, AdCP and AAMP — and keeps every claim sourced and dated.

Updated 4 August 2026 · every claim on this site was verified against its sources on that date

Claims are badged by evidence strength; unbadged statements are verified as shipped.

  • shipped released or in production
  • announced committed, not yet shipped
  • reported secondary sources only, or disputed
  • speculative interpretation, not settled fact

What changed

  1. 4 August 2026

    AdCP tagged release v3.1.10, continuing a weekly-plus cadence on the v3.1.x line through July and August. A parallel v3.0.x maintenance line is still receiving releases alongside it (v3.0.22 on 24 July). Release v3.1.3 was withdrawn during that run for adding a schema field in a patch release, which the project states violates its 3.1.x release contract; the documentation pins 3.1.2 as the stable 3.1 target.

    Source: AdCP GitHub releases · last verified 2026-08-04

  2. 1 August 2026

    AdCP v2 was fully deprecated. The specification documentation states that v2 became unsupported when 3.0 reached general availability and was fully deprecated on this date; 3.1 is the current stable minor version and is additive over 3.0, so a 3.0-conformant client keeps working.

    Source: AdCP documentation — what's new in 3.0; what's new in 3.1 · last verified 2026-08-04

  3. 30 July 2026

    IAB Tech Lab released AAMP 2.3, aimed at enterprise deployment: pricing guardrails with server-side trust verification on every price-moving path, a vendor-approval gate in the Buyer Agent tied to the IAB Diligence Platform and SafeGuard Privacy, Agentic Audiences v1.0 ready for transactions, and broader platform support — Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, Databricks, buying on Meta, and Google Ad Manager reporting.

    Source: PR Newswire; PPC Land; MediaPost · last verified 2026-08-04

  4. 8 July 2026

    announced Adform publicly reaffirmed its commitment to open standards for agentic advertising, without naming a protocol implementation timeline.

    Source: ExchangeWire · last verified 2026-08-04

  5. 24 June 2026

    DanAds and Sigma Software launched a Sales Agent for Publishers built natively on AdCP, mapping DanAds' existing inventory, packaging, and permission model onto AdCP primitives. It launched with theScore and RTL AdAlliance, and RTL AdAlliance put a seller agent on its AdManager platform to give agents access to its European Total Video inventory.

    Source: ExchangeWire · last verified 2026-08-04

  6. 19 June 2026

    Two buy-side launches in the same week: WPP released a Buyer Agent for Video, an AI-driven planning tool, and Yahoo DSP announced an Agent Network of 23 partners. Neither announcement states which agentic protocol it uses.

    Source: MarTech Edition · last verified 2026-08-04

  7. 17 June 2026

    Microsoft Advertising shipped an official MCP server, read-only: agents can read live campaign data, but changes still require a human. Pinterest also launched an ads MCP server.

    Source: Digital Applied · last verified 2026-08-04

  8. 7 April 2026

    PMG integrated AAMP's Buyer Agent architecture, Sub-Agent architecture, and Agent Registry into its Alli operating system, which the agency uses to manage media buying, integrate data providers, and coordinate with demand-side platforms. It is the first AAMP deployment tracked here that runs in an agency's production environment rather than a test workspace.

    Source: PR Newswire · last verified 2026-08-04

  9. 12 March 2026

    Optable's Audience Agent was integrated into PubMatic AgenticOS, letting buying agents discover and activate publisher first-party audience signals without the underlying data being transferred. The companies position it as an early live example of AdCP running across production programmatic infrastructure, with activation through PubMatic Activate.

    Source: PPC Land; Optable · last verified 2026-08-04

  10. 15 December 2025

    PubMatic and agency Butler/Till ran an AdCP-enabled agentic campaign across connected TV for the Clubtails brand, planned, executed, and optimized by agents with no demand-side platform workflow and no manual optimization desk. PubMatic describes AdCP as the protocol carrying agent-to-system communication in that workflow.

    Source: PubMatic; MediaPost · last verified 2026-08-04

Older developments — AdCP's launch, the first agent-to-agent buy, the NBCUniversal cross-platform buys, AAMP 2.0, and Kochava's open beta — are kept in the timeline tables on the AdCP and AAMP pages.

How this site is maintained

An agentic development workflow builds this site: research agents verify developments against primary sources, writing agents turn them into neutral copy, and every change is reviewed before publication. Because this space moves monthly, each claim records the date it was last checked rather than implying it is permanently current.
