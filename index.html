How claims are labeled

Every claim on this site carries a source link and the date it was last verified. Where the evidence is weaker than a verified, shipped capability, the claim also carries a badge; unbadged statements are verified as shipped.

  • shipped released or in production
  • announced committed, not yet shipped
  • reported secondary sources only, or disputed
  • speculative interpretation, not settled fact

The site is built and maintained by an agentic development workflow: research agents verify developments against primary sources, writing agents turn them into neutral copy, and every change is reviewed before publication.
