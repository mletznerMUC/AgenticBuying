Agentic Buying, explained and tracked

AI agents are beginning to discover, negotiate, and transact media buys on behalf of advertisers. This site tracks that shift — with a focus on the two emerging protocol families, AdCP and AAMP — and keeps every claim sourced and dated.

Updated 4 August 2026 · every claim on this site was verified against its sources on that date

Claims are badged by evidence strength; unbadged statements are verified as shipped.

  • shipped released or in production
  • announced committed, not yet shipped
  • reported secondary sources only, or disputed
  • speculative interpretation, not settled fact

What changed

  1. 4 August 2026

    AdCP tagged release v3.1.10, continuing a weekly-plus cadence on the v3.1.x line through July and August. Release v3.1.3 was withdrawn earlier in that run for a semantic-versioning violation.

    Source: AdCP GitHub releases · last verified 2026-08-04

  2. 30 July 2026

    IAB Tech Lab released AAMP 2.3, aimed at enterprise deployment: a pricing-provenance field so agents cannot invent prices, a vendor-approval gate in the Buyer Agent tied to the IAB Diligence Platform and SafeGuard Privacy, OpenRTB-based audience embeddings with a Prebid module, and compatibility with Amazon Bedrock and Databricks.

    Source: PR Newswire; PPC Land; MediaPost · last verified 2026-08-04

  3. 8 July 2026

    announced Adform publicly reaffirmed its commitment to open standards for agentic advertising, without naming a protocol implementation timeline.

    Source: ExchangeWire · last verified 2026-08-04

  4. 17 June 2026

    Microsoft Advertising shipped an official MCP server, read-only: agents can read live campaign data, but changes still require a human. Pinterest also launched an ads MCP server.

    Source: Digital Applied · last verified 2026-08-04

  5. April 2026

    AAMP 2.0 delivered transaction-ready Buyer and Seller Agent SDKs built on OpenDirect 2.1, able to negotiate, share rate cards, and execute deals with audit trails and human-approval guardrails. reported Sources conflict on the date: IAB Tech Lab and LexisNexis coverage indicate April 2026, while one secondary source says June; April is the better-sourced date.

    Source: IAB Tech Lab; LexisNexis · last verified 2026-08-04

  6. 24 March 2026

    Kochava put the AAMP Buyer Agent into open beta through its open-source StationOne "IAB Workspace," letting buyers test standards-compliant agentic workflows on OpenDirect 2.1 without committing real spend.

    Source: GlobeNewswire; PPC Land · last verified 2026-08-04

  7. 6 January 2026

    NBCUniversal, agency RPA, FreeWheel, and Newton Research announced the first cross-platform agentic media buys: buy-side agents negotiated with sell-side agents over MCP, including live NFL playoff linear inventory.

    Source: NBCUniversal · last verified 2026-08-04

How this site is maintained

An agentic development workflow builds this site: research agents verify developments against primary sources, writing agents turn them into neutral copy, and every change is reviewed before publication. Because this space moves monthly, each claim records the date it was last checked rather than implying it is permanently current.
